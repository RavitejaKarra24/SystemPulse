## Direction and research

Keep SystemPulse a private, lightweight native macOS menu-bar utility, not a web dashboard. Borrow visual principles rather than copy another product's assets.

Sources reviewed for this revamp:
- [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/): public design-pattern library and homepage. Inspiration: clear hierarchy, generous spacing, neutral surfaces, recognizable navigation, and explicit states. Private/login-only screen collections were not reviewed.
- [iStat Menus](https://bjango.com/mac/istatmenus/): per-core CPU, memory/swap, history, configurable rules/alerts, disk activity, network details, battery and customization.
- [Stats](https://github.com/exelban/stats): modular monitoring, top consumers, menu-bar customization; sensor/platform limitations and energy cost are important constraints.
- [DaisyDisk](https://daisydiskapp.com/): storage drill-down, previews, deliberate cleanup and safety stops.

Prioritize frequent, actionable needs: understand resource use, find the responsible app, spot pressure early, reclaim space deliberately. Do not add weather, clocks, cloud dashboards, automatic cleaners, or fan control just for feature parity. No fake telemetry, unimplemented buttons, silent privilege escalation, or public-IP requests without opt-in.
