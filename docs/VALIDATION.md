## Known boundaries

- Memory pressure remains a headroom estimate, explicitly labelled. It is not Activity Monitor's exact pressure signal.
- Network throughput covers physical interfaces; it is not per-app attribution or proof that the internet is reachable. Legacy 32-bit interface counters can lose one wrap interval.
- Disk capacity is APFS available-for-important-usage space; logical scanned file sizes are not a guarantee of immediate physical space recovery. Unmeasured locations are counted, not yet itemized by path/reason. Cancelled or unmeasured scans are read-only; a complete rescan is required before cleanup. Cancellation is cooperative between filesystem calls, not a guarantee of immediate interruption. Cleanup scans are targeted; selected-folder inventory is separate and always read-only. Selected-folder totals include hidden/package files but may count hard links more than once; neither mode is an atomic filesystem snapshot or a physical-space recovery guarantee.
- Cleanup is restricted to eligible cache roots, validated again at the backend. Non-cache support/container/archival/restoration data is read-only. There is no permanent deletion, elevation, automatic retry/cleanup or promised in-app Undo. Queue selection is bounded and requires itemized review plus explicit confirmation; Stop/normal Quit cannot interrupt an OS call already in flight. Forced termination/crashes cannot guarantee recorded outcomes. Failed or skipped locations require a fresh review and may need removal or rescan if they are no longer eligible.
- PID reuse between measurement and user action, and filesystem races between validation and Trash/Quick Look/Finder, remain hardening concerns. Scope/entry checks revalidate path containment, type and symlinks, not an atomic handle-locked or inode-preserving filesystem snapshot. Same-path replacement after scanning may display newer content.
- Whole-machine power and optional battery properties depend on hardware/macOS support. Legacy cycle/capacity/battery-temperature/power detail uses hardware-specific registry fields, not a stable cross-hardware sensor contract; unit/accuracy validation on other Macs remains open. [GPU, additional sensor/fan and accessory-battery feasibility](HARDWARE_FEASIBILITY.md) is completed, but those optional backends are not implemented and are deferred from this release. Public thermal state is a system category, not temperature.
- The current prebuilt archive is arm64/Apple Silicon, not universal. Intel hardware remains untested in this phase; Intel users need a source build.
- Launch at Login may fail or require approval for a local ad-hoc-signed bundle. The app now reports this rather than swallowing it.
- History is in memory only: 1,201 raw samples, 361 ten-second buckets and 1,441 minute buckets per series. Hour/day points are sample-weighted means timestamped at their last actual observation; boundary-straddling buckets are omitted. Displayed coarse peaks are peaks of means, not original spikes. Raw lines leave gaps over 4.5 seconds (15 seconds for the slower power sampler). Network/disk counter baselines initially display zero before a second sample establishes a rate. CPU baselines are omitted from current numeric summaries, clipboard, diagnostics, legacy sparklines, timed charts, menu-bar and alert evidence until a real interval exists. On sleep, observed byte totals and history are retained but suspended bytes are not counted or smeared into a post-wake rate; shutdown invalidates polling and cannot be reversed by wake/settings callbacks.
- Selected-network history begins when selected and resets on switching. Disconnected metadata/session totals are bounded to five minutes and 16 removed interfaces; eviction resets that interface's session baseline. Active is a link state, not Internet reachability.
- Export captures latest-known fields once when a format is chosen; it does not resample or follow subsequent UI changes. JSON history carries actual sample timestamps; CSV explicitly distinguishes metadata, snapshot capture timestamps and history timestamps with `recordType`. Its fixed five-minute history covers CPU, memory, aggregate network and system power, not every chart/field. Unavailable JSON values are omitted; CSV values are blank. Private identities, processes, paths, volume names/IDs and interface addresses are excluded. No automatic export or on-disk history.
- Selected volume metadata refreshes only while the panel is open, normally every ten seconds plus mount notifications. It may briefly lag mount changes. Missing selected capacity stays unavailable, never silently substituted with home-volume numbers. Sampling is metadata-only, not a volume scan.
- Phase 3 rules are observation-based, not continuous physical measurements. New actual source timestamps must cover ≥30 seconds with gaps ≤10 seconds; cached points cannot advance a window. Cooldown is ≥15 minutes and valid recovery is required to rearm. State is in memory and resets on relaunch/disable; a sustained condition never warns immediately at startup. Effective per-rule/session tokens invalidate transient edits/opt-outs and queued deliveries; unrelated rule changes retain their windows. Individual acquisition times are checked against application-time wall clock, not re-dated to hide actor/I/O/sleep delays. Workspace sleep invalidates outstanding result generations; wake resets rate/CPU/process baselines. Alert pending windows restart, but a latched breach/cooldown is retained: sleep and baseline-only CPU values are not recovery. Failed/truncated VM statistics remain unavailable rather than false zero headroom.
- Home capacity is additionally sampled while closed for a disk menu metric (about 10s) or enabled/authorized disk alert (about 5s, rounded to polling passes). This does not redirect volume selection or cleanup, and is metadata-only. The release probe matrix measures CPU/footprint/wakeup proxies for these paths separately; the actual packaged-app energy budget and long-session behavior remain unverified.
- Notifications require the installed .app and explicit authorization. OS settings/Focus control presentation; failures are exposed in Settings, not silently retried or treated as successful delivery. A failed, superseded or expired delivery still consumes that breach event/cooldown and requires recovery before a fresh event. Events older than 10 seconds (or future/invalid timestamps) are dropped before delivery, including after suspended authorization queries. Concurrent breaches share one query; old queries cannot overwrite explicit authorization results, and a slow older delivery success cannot hide a newer error. Notification/login access is guarded outside application bundles and inside XCTest. Ad-hoc rebuilds may change OS permission behavior; upgrade checks remain open.
- Insights report sample-weighted CPU means or fresh snapshots with thresholds/caveats, not diagnoses, causes, temperatures or proof of current memory pressure. Process names appear locally only, never in notifications/exports. Empty results make no blanket health claim.
- Phase 3 functionality is implemented; its actual-notification, manual/hardware and energy gates remain open. Phase 4 has started with cancellable cleanup and read-only selected-folder inventory. Drill-down/Quick Look implementation and automated checks are completed; its actual-preview/interaction gates remain open. Reviewed cleanup queue implementation/automated checks are completed in 1.6.3, but actual Trash/restore/Stop/Quit/accessibility/resource gates remain not started. Hardware feasibility and the static source/limit disclosure are completed; optional expansion is explicitly deferred and not implemented. At that earlier checkpoint, Phase 5 native accessibility source/automated checks were completed and its assistive/visual-setting gate was open; it is now withdrawn, not passed. Current ordinary keyboard/interaction, controlled packaged-app energy/soak and fresh-install/upgrade gates remain pending as recorded above. No release-readiness percentage is inferred.
